```markdown
# Ahrens, *How to Take Smart Notes* (2nd ed., 2022)

Read: 2026-07. Wanted principles to stop my vault sprawling.

## Ideas that struck me (my words, with location)
- Structure should emerge from links between atomic notes, not be imposed as a
  folder hierarchy up front.
- Writing an idea in your own words is the test of whether you understand it;
  familiarity from re-reading is not understanding (mere-exposure effect).
- Project notes are separate from the slipbox and are thrown away when the
  project ends; the slipbox is what persists.

## Feeds
- [[structure-should-emerge-from-links-not-folders]]
- [[familiarity-is-not-understanding]]
- [[project-notes-are-disposable-the-slipbox-persists]]
```
